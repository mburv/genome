How to playSwipe a tile left or right to rotate its entire row, or up or down to rotate its column. The tiles wrap around the edges. Match the target in the outlined middle row, from the left. The final HBB fragment has just three bases. You can also tap a tile and use the arrow buttons or keyboard. Hint shows a reversible move towards the solution; undo reverses your previous move. Every board is scrambled from a solved board.
